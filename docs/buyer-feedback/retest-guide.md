# Buyer wording retest

Use this short follow-up only with real participants after the revised site is deployed. Do not fill in outcomes in advance.

## Record before starting

- Date and approximate time
- General buyer role only; do not record a name, phone number or business address
- Device type and browser
- Exact website URL and tested commit
- Whether the participant saw the earlier wording
- Anonymous-publication consent (`yes` or `no`)

## Four tasks

Do not explain a term until the participant has answered.

1. On **Today**, ask: “What does the middle estimate mean, and what does the possible range mean?”
2. Show a **no clear signal** example and ask: “Does this mean the price did not change, or something else?”
3. On **Explore**, ask: “What is a day when both prices were available, and what does 110 mean when the chart starts at 100?”
4. On **Weekly report**, point to a blank value and ask: “What does this blank mean?”

For each task, record `completed without help`, `completed with help` or `not completed`, approximate time, and the participant's explanation in their own words.

## Decision rule

The wording revision passes when both participants complete all four tasks without help and neither interprets the possible range as a guarantee. If either participant needs help, revise only the confusing sentence and repeat that task with a different buyer. Report all attempted sessions, including unsuccessful ones.

This is a comprehension retest, not evidence of economic impact or realised savings.
