# Buyer feedback study

This folder is the evidence pack for testing Price Signal with **2 to 3 real small-food buyers** before the final report and video. The study tests whether buyers understand and trust the cards; it does not claim that the model changes real purchase outcomes.

**Completed:** three anonymous sessions were conducted on 16-17 October 2026. The project owner confirmed consent to publish the anonymous responses on 18 October. Individual records are in `responses/`; aggregated results, product decisions and study limitations are in `findings-summary.md`.

## Who to recruit

Recruit people who regularly decide when or where to buy vegetables for a canteen, restaurant, grocery, market stall or similar small business. Use participant IDs (`B01`, `B02`, `B03`) in public notes. Do not publish names, telephone numbers, business addresses or commercially sensitive purchase figures.

## What to do

1. Open the frozen version of the site on a laptop or phone. A local copy is acceptable before public deployment.
2. Read the consent statement in `session-guide.md` and obtain verbal consent.
3. Let the participant perform the five tasks without coaching. Record task completion, time, misunderstandings and short quotations.
4. Copy `response-template.md` to `responses/B01.md` (and so on) and fill it in immediately after each session.
5. After at least two sessions, complete `findings-summary.md`. Count only observed results; do not infer missing answers.
6. Fix only high-impact problems that block understanding or safe use. Repeat the functionality-freeze checks after any functional change.

## Minimum evidence for the submission

- Two completed, anonymised response files; three is better.
- Task success counts and the trust/usefulness ratings.
- At least one concrete change made from feedback, or an honest statement that no change was justified.
- A dated findings summary describing who was tested, how the test ran, limitations and unresolved concerns.

Keep raw notes private if they contain identifying details. Publish only the anonymised responses and summary that participants agreed could be used for the student project.
