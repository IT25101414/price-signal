Quick answers

What am I looking at?

For each vegetable or fruit, the page compares its Dambulla wholesale price with a Colombo price and shows the estimated change over the 3 report days after the dated source report, with a range. A report day is a day the Central Bank publishes a price report, so a weekend is skipped.

Should I buy or wait because of this?

This page never tells you to buy or sell. The estimates are statistical and the ranges are wide; a change of a few percent is often smaller than the uncertainty. Use it as one more piece of information next to what you see at the market.

Why does an item show no figures?

Because the page would rather say nothing than something unreliable. The table below lists every reason and how many items are affected right now.

Why is the whole page withheld?

The latest Central Bank report may be more than 3 working days old, the Central Bank site may list a newer report that has not been read yet, or the latest refresh may have been unable to check the source. Estimates return only after the source is checked and the data are current. Public holidays do not count as working days.

Where do the prices come from?

The Daily Price Report of the Statistics Department, Central Bank of Sri Lanka. Prices are rounded to the nearest Rs 5 or 10 in the report, so cheap items move in steps.

Does the Central Bank back this project?

No. The data are theirs; the analysis and this website are ours. The Central Bank bears no responsibility for the project or its findings and does not endorse them.

How good are the estimates?

The Method page shows the evidence: how much Dambulla adds, whether the 80% range holds up, and what a simulated buyer would have saved. The saving was modest, 1.2 to 1.6% of the purchase value in the test.

Does the page work with a screen reader or keyboard?

Yes. Everything can be reached with the keyboard, direction is shown with arrows and signs as well as colour, and every chart has a table version under it. The Theme button switches between light and dark.

Glossary

Report day
A day with a Central Bank price report. Weekends and listed public holidays are skipped.
Dambulla wholesale
The wholesale price at the Dambulla dedicated economic centre, the main source of vegetables for Colombo.
Colombo price
One of three prices from the same report: wholesale at Pettah, retail at Pettah, or retail at Narahenpita.
Colombo vs its usual gap
How far today's Colombo price sits from where it normally is relative to Dambulla. Zero means a normal gap.
Middle estimate
The model's single best guess for the change in the Colombo price over the 3 report days after the dated source report.
80% range
The range that held the real change about 8 times in 10 when we checked on past prices. Wide ranges mean the model is unsure.
Shared report day
A date when both Dambulla and the selected Colombo market published a price for the item. Comparisons use these shared dates so the markets are matched fairly.
Starts at 100
A chart view for comparing movement rather than rupee levels. Each market begins at 100 on the first shared date; 110 means 10% above its own starting price.
Blank weekly value
At least one of the five daily prices needed for that weekly average was unavailable. A blank is not treated as zero.
Strong and weak signal
How large the middle estimate is compared with the model's typical estimate. Strong does not mean certain; the range still applies.
Withheld
The page chose not to show a figure. The reason is always given.

Report a problem or ask a question

If a price looks wrong, a page does not load, or something here is unclear, tell us on the project's issue page. Please include the report date shown at the top of the page, the item and the Colombo price, and what you expected to see. If you cannot use that page, the project author is listed in the repository README.

For anything about the Central Bank's reports themselves, contact the Statistics Department of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka directly.

Limits

These are statistical estimates from past prices, not forecasts you can rely on, and not financial or business advice. The site was built for a student competition and is not an official service.
