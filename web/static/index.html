        How to read this

        Each bar shows the likely range for the Colombo price change during the three report days after the dated source report. The mark inside the bar is the best single estimate. A longer bar means the result is more uncertain.

        Past reports show that Dambulla wholesale prices often move before Colombo prices. These estimates are uncertain and should be used as one piece of information, not as instructions to buy, wait or sell. The data are published by the Statistics Department, Central Bank of Sri Lanka, which bears no responsibility for and does not endorse this project or its findings.
