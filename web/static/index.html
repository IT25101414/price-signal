        How to read this

        Each bar is the model's 80% range for the change in the Colombo price over the 3 report days after the dated source report. The mark inside it is the middle estimate. All bars share one scale, so a longer bar means more uncertainty.

        Dambulla wholesale prices have led Colombo prices in past reports. These are statistical estimates from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka's Daily Price Reports, not forecasts you can rely on, and not advice. The data are published by the Statistics Department, Central Bank of Sri Lanka, which bears no responsibility for and does not endorse this project or its findings.
