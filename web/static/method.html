Price Signal

Method and evidence

What the estimates are, how they are made, and how well they did on prices the model had not seen.

The idea

Most of Colombo's fresh produce passes through Dambulla first. When the Dambulla wholesale price moves a lot, Colombo prices have tended to follow over the next few report days. Price Signal estimates how much of the current gap is likely to close, and how unsure that estimate is.

  1. Every working day the Central Bank's Daily Price Report is read, table by table, and added to the price history. A report that cannot be read is left out, never guessed.
  2. For each item and each Colombo price, a regression model compares the source report's Dambulla-to-Colombo gap with its own usual level and with the recent moves of both prices, and estimates the change over the following 3 report days.
  3. The 80% range comes from how wrong the model was in a year it was not fitted on. It is wide on purpose.
  4. A guard checks the data again on every page view. If the report is more than 3 working days old, a newer report exists that has not been read, a market was closed, a price is missing, there is too little history, or the signal is too small, the page shows the reason and no number.

Is the 80% range honest?

Share of outcomes that landed inside the stated 80% range. The shaded band is the 75 to 85% we set as acceptable before looking.

Would it have saved money?

A simulated buyer buys once, today or 3 report days later. Cost against always buying today, as a share of the purchase value; below zero is cheaper. The hindsight bar is a ceiling no real buyer can reach.

What this does not show

  • It is a simulation on past prices. It assumes a purchase can wait a few days at no cost, which small kitchens and perishable produce may not allow.
  • The test period, 2024 onward, was calm. The effect may weaken in a crisis like 2022, which sits in the training data.
  • The effect is modest and covers only the 18 to 19 items that have a Dambulla wholesale price.
  • No buyer has used the cards yet, so whether people would act on them is not tested.
